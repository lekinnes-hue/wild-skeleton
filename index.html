Set up your game

A local pass-and-play game · No sign-in needed

How to play

1Choose truth or dare when it’s your turn.
2The group submits prompts. The moderator picks one.
3Complete it or pass. Tally points for five rounds.

Keep it comfortable

Passing is always okay. The moderator can skip anything that feels unsafe, too personal, or simply not fun.

Points

+2 complete a prompt+1 picked prompt0 pass
